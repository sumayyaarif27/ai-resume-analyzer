We are looking for a Data Analyst to join our team in Bengaluru.

Responsibilities:

- Strong knowledge of SQL and Excel
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau
- Basic Python (Pandas) skills
- Good communication and problem-solving skills
- Knowledge of statistics is a plus
