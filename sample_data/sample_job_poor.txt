Job Title: Staff Nurse - Intensive Care Unit (ICU)

Location: Bengaluru

About the role:
We are hiring a Staff Nurse for our 30-bed Intensive Care Unit. You will provide direct bedside care to critically ill patients under the supervision of the ICU consultant.

Responsibilities:
- Monitor vital signs and manage ventilators, infusion pumps and cardiac monitors
- Administer medications and IV fluids as prescribed
- Perform wound care, catheter care and emergency resuscitation
- Maintain accurate patient charts and hand over shifts clearly
- Support families with compassion and clear communication
- Work rotating day and night shifts, including weekends

Requirements:
- B.Sc Nursing or GNM from a recognized institution
- Valid registration with the State Nursing Council
- Minimum 2 years of ICU or critical care experience
- Current BLS and ACLS certification
- Ability to stay calm and act quickly in emergencies
